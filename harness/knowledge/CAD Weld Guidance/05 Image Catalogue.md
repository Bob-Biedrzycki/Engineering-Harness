# Image catalogue

Every figure in `images/`, one folder per source, with what it shows. Downloaded 2026-11-19 for CPME's internal
engineering reference (Bob, chat, 2026-11-19: "we are not commercially soliciting them we are simply using them for
reference"). Copyright stays with each publisher (credited per folder); do not copy these into anything CPME sends out.
The `open-textbook` set is CC BY 4.0 and may be reused with its credit line.

Most useful for our stainless tube frame, in order: **AWS slotted-HSS figures 1, 8, 9, 10**; **STRUCTURE "Know Your HSS
Welds" figures 2, 4, 8**; **STRUCTURE "Misapplied" figures 2 to 4**; **Miller 04, 07, 08, 18**; **GoEngineer weld vs
fillet bead 02, 04, 08, 09**.
