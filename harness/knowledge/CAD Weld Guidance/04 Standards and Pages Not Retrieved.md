# Standards and pages not retrieved

The governing standards are paid, and some free pages refuse automated reads. What is known about each, from search
results only (2026-11-19), is below. Nothing here is from the documents themselves.

| Source | Why not here | What search results said |
|---|---|---|
| **AWS A2.4:2020**, Standard Symbols for Welding, Brazing and Nondestructive Examination | Paid standard (AWS / ANSI webstore) | Current edition 2020 (earlier 2007, 2012). Covers groove, fillet, plug, slot, spot, seam, edge, stud and surfacing symbols, plus NDE symbols, with worked examples. All-around is not needed on a round joint. Repeated identical symbols may be replaced by one symbol marked typical with the count, arrow at a representative joint; "TYP" only for truly identical welds. |
| ANSI blog, A2.4-2020 changes. https://blog.ansi.org/ansi/standard-welding-symbol-change-aws-a2-4-2020/ | Refused (403) | Summary of what changed in the 2020 edition. Not read. |
| **AWS D1.6/D1.6M:2017** (with 2021 amendment), Structural Welding Code, Stainless Steel | Paid standard; AWS scope PDF removed (404); ANSI blog and AWS forum refused (403) | Covers stainless structural welding other than pressure vessels and piping; written because D1.1 does not cover stainless. A 2005 forum post said D1.6-1999 had no minimum fillet size table like D1.1's, only a maximum. A LANL procedure built on D1.6 says drawings should show location, type, size and extent of every weld and separate shop from field welds. **Check the 2017 text for fillet size limits before writing our general note.** |
| AISC Modern Steel, Steelwise, July 2007. https://www.aisc.org/globalassets/modern-steel/archives/2007/07/2007v07_steelwise.pdf | AISC refuses automated downloads (403); its figures are **not** in `images/`. Open the link in a browser and save the PDF into this folder by hand. | Fillet welds are taken as continuous unless noted; examples of correct "typical" use in structural details. |
| **AISC Design Guide 24**, Hollow Structural Section Connections, 2nd ed. (2024, J. Packer) | Product page refused (403); guide is free to AISC members only | The HSS weld design and detailing reference; effective weld length for square/rectangular tube per AISC 360-22 Table K5.1/K5.2. |
| AISC Steel Construction Manual, Table 8-2 | Paid | The standard table of prequalified weld symbols used in US structural detailing. |
| ASME Y14.41-2019 | Paid (ASME) | See `03 Welds in the 3D Model.md` entry F for the secondary summary. |

Also without figures: the SOLIDWORKS 2017 Weld Beads help page (no article content came through) and Miller's
"Deciphering weld symbols" (page removed; the other Miller article's 21 figures and symbol chart cover it).
